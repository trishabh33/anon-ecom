Returns Policy

1. Overview

Your satisfaction matters to us. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, we're here to help. Please review the following policy to determine if your order qualifies for a return, exchange, or refund.

2. Eligible Returns

  • Returns must be requested within 30 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused, in their original condition, and with all packaging and tags intact.
  • A valid proof of purchase (e.g., order confirmation or invoice) is required.

3. Exceptions & Non-Returnable Items

  • Clearance or final sale items.
  • Perishable goods (e.g., food, plants, flowers).
  • Opened personal items such as cosmetics, underwear, or earbuds due to hygiene reasons.
  • Customized or personalized products.

If you're unsure about your item, please contact our support team before returning.

4. Return Process

  1. Submit a Request: Email us at returns@example.com with your order number, product name, and reason for return.
  2. Approval: Our team will review your request within 1–2 business days and respond with next steps and return address.
  3. Ship the Item: Package the item securely and include any required paperwork. Shipping is at the customer’s expense unless otherwise stated.

5. Refunds & Processing

Once your return is received, we will:

  • Inspect the item within 3–5 business days.
  • If approved, initiate a refund to your original payment method within 2 business days.
  • Refunds may take up to 5–10 business days to appear depending on your bank.

Note: A restocking fee may apply on high-value items (5–10%), unless the return is due to our error.

6. Exchanges

If you'd like a replacement or size exchange, please indicate this in your return request. Exchanges are subject to stock availability and processed once the original item is returned.
