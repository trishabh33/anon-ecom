About Fivizo

Fivizo is a next-gen e-commerce platform designed exclusively for the bold, expressive, and digitally native Gen Z community. Founded by Avyansh Katiyar, Fivizo was born out of a desire to create a shopping experience that’s not only seamless and fast—but deeply in tune with the identity and culture of a new generation of shoppers.

Funny thing is, the idea for Fivizo actually struck Avyansh while he was rage-scrolling through a dozen different online stores at 2 AM, trying to find a pair of socks that matched his vibe. Somewhere between “galaxy print” and “chicken nugget pattern,” he realized—Gen Z deserves better. So he closed his 43 open tabs, grabbed a notebook, and wrote: "Make a store that doesn’t suck." That note became the blueprint for Fivizo.

We offer a curated selection of trendy fashion, cutting-edge gadgets, lifestyle accessories, and creative essentials—all handpicked to match the unique tastes and dynamic needs of Gen Z. Whether you're a trendsetter, content creator, student, gamer, or simply someone who values style and authenticity, Fivizo is built for you.

What makes us different? Really ? At Fivizo, we don't just sell products—we empower identity. With intuitive design, lightning-fast checkout, affordable pricing, and community-driven collections, we're reimagining what it means to shop online in the 2020s.

Our Mission

To become the go-to e-commerce hub for Gen Z by delivering products and experiences that reflect their values—creativity, individuality, speed, and impact.
